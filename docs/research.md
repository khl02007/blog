# Research

My research asks how distributed neural circuits work together to transform sensory information into representations that support cognition and behavior. I combine systems neuroscience, neural technology, and open computational tools.

## Hippocampal-cortical circuits

I study how cognitive processes influence visual processing and how hippocampal and cortical circuits coordinate.

## Neural recording technology

I develop and apply novel electrode technologies, including flexible neural probes and electrode-pooling approaches for large-scale extracellular recordings.

## Open and reproducible neuroscience

I contribute to open tools and workflows—including Spyglass—that make complex neuroscience analyses reproducible and shareable across laboratories.
